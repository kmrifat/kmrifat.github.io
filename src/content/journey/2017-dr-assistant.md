It began as a JavaFX desktop application in 2017 and became the product line I
keep returning to: the Laravel rewrite, then Dr Assistant PRO, and most recently
a full SaaS version with a separate API and frontend.

I designed the database, built the frontend and the application itself, and wrote
the product documentation.
