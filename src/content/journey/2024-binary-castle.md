Binary Castle is where the product ideas and the reusable tooling live. Much of
it is deliberately built as libraries first — form, table, page-builder and
admin-panel packages that the products then consume — so each new product starts
further along than the last.

The Django backend serving this portfolio runs on Binary Castle infrastructure.
